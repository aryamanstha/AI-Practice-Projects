Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming various industries, from healthcare to finance. In healthcare, AI-powered algorithms assist doctors in diagnosing diseases more accurately and at an early stage. Additionally, AI-driven robotic surgeries are becoming more precise, reducing recovery times for patients. In finance, AI helps detect fraudulent transactions and provides personalized investment advice. With advancements in deep learning and natural language processing, AI is continuously evolving. However, ethical concerns such as data privacy and bias in AI models remain significant challenges
